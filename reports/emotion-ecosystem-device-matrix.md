Automated context-loss verification dispatches the WebGL context events in Chromium and checks local fallback/recovery and camera controls. It does not force an actual driver-level GPU reset. Pointer verification compares rendered canvas output after drag with reduced motion enabled; it confirms a visible disturbance in automation, not touch behavior on a physical phone.

## Required real-device record

On each target device, capture model SHA, browser/OS version, network conditions, cold and warm first usable time, baseline model transfer and scene chunk transfer, 200+ classification samples with P50/P95 detection/preprocess/classify/end-to-end latency, page and scene FPS, and CPU/GPU/memory pressure. Run camera and scene together for 10 minutes and stability/memory observation for 30 minutes, including background/foreground and reduced-motion checks. Keep physical device measurements separate from viewport emulation and headless browser results.
